# Evaluation Rubric

Score each module from 1 to 5.

## Criteria

- Central-thesis fit: does it support geometry-dependent memory architecture?
- Novelty: does it add something beyond maps/graphs, SR, TEM, or sequence-first theories?
- Formal clarity: are the variables operationally defined?
- Biological plausibility: is the claim defensible without overclaiming anatomy?
- Experimental testability: can it produce concrete simulations or experiments?
- Lab continuity: does it naturally extend Leibold lab work?
- Modular independence: can the module be revised without rewriting the whole project?
- Risk control: does it avoid making Dyck, hyperbolicity, or partial commutation do too much?

## Acceptance Threshold

A module is manuscript-ready when it scores at least 4 on central-thesis fit, formal clarity, experimental testability, and risk control.
